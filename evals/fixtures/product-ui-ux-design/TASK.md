State these limitations explicitly. Do not invent users, measurements, requirements, observed runtime behavior, or accessibility conformance.

Prioritize findings, distinguish verified fixture evidence from heuristics and hypotheses, model the critical states, and add observable acceptance criteria for a later implementation.

Define the experience-performance boundary for the critical tasks without inventing timing measurements or a technical root cause. State what later field, laboratory, browser, and physical-device evidence would be needed; do not turn the audit into profiling or optimization work.
